# The Farmer creates the Twitch Rewards

Twitch lets an app change a Reward, pause it, and settle its Redemptions only for Rewards that app's `client_id` created. The Farmer chooses which Rewards to offer and creates only those, under fixed titles recognised by the extension. The Farmer also completes or refunds Redemptions in Twitch. The extension honours the Pet request without calling Twitch to confirm or cancel it, even if a Reward was originally created by this app. Creating selected Rewards through the app to enable automatic confirmations and refunds was considered; manual creation and settlement were chosen instead. See [Update Redemption Status](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/#update-redemption-status).

Each Specie has its own optional add Reward, such as «Добавить кота в IDE» and «Добавить собаку в IDE». The Reward selects the Specie; the Viewer's text selects only its Variant. An empty choice or `random` selects a random Variant. Dino, Duck, Raccoon and Horse have no Variants, so their Rewards require no text. The optional «Удалить питомца из IDE» Reward still removes one of the Viewer's own Pets. The fixed titles are listed in [issue #2](https://github.com/knitleria/Stardew-Pets/issues/2), and the add behaviour in [issue #3](https://github.com/knitleria/Stardew-Pets/issues/3).

The Farmer enables Twitch's text-input setting for Rewards with Variants and prompts the Viewer for a Variant or `random`. Twitch requires text when this setting is enabled, so `random` makes the random choice available in the standard redemption form. Empty text received in a Redemption is also accepted. The Farmer disables text input for Species without Variants and for removal. See the [Twitch API reference](https://dev.twitch.tv/docs/api/reference/#get-custom-reward-redemption).

## Consequences

Every Reward is optional, including removal. Any subset of the 17 supported titles is valid, including none. Missing, disabled or paused Rewards cause no toast, warning, error or request to create them, and do not prevent connection or other Rewards from working. The Farmer can omit Junimo, for example, without the extension complaining.

The Farmer can create, delete, enable, disable, pause or edit Rewards in Twitch at any time, including while the channel is offline. Recognition uses the current title in each Redemption, so a newly created or re-created Reward with a supported title works without reconnecting, regardless of its id at startup. An unknown title is ignored silently; renaming to a supported title lets subsequent Redemptions work again.

The extension does not create a Reward, and does not change its cost, prompt, text-input setting, enabled state, pause or queue. Connection, reconnection and stream status do not override the Farmer's settings. Removing or disabling a Reward does not remove existing Pets. While this window is connected it listens for Redemptions whether or not the channel is live. Redemptions that piled up while the extension was down are not fetched. Channel points exist only on Affiliate and Partner channels; everyone else gets a `403` and no Twitch features.

The extension never writes Redemption statuses and never refunds channel points automatically. A valid add or removal changes the shared farm immediately; no Twitch confirmation is needed. A rejected request, including invalid Variant, farm or Viewer limit, or removal without an owned Pet, leaves the farm unchanged and logs the reason with a reminder to refund manually. A save failure is reported with the same reminder. Successful farm changes and greetings do not claim that the Twitch queue was completed. The Farmer can leave the request queue enabled so both confirmation and refunds remain available in Twitch; the extension does not change that setting. Manually completing or refunding a Redemption later does not replay or undo a farm change.
